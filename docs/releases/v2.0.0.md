# v2.0.0 — Software Research Release

## Summary

v2.0.0 is the repository's Stage-22 software research baseline for GPS-denied UAV swarm autonomy. It consolidates the cross-platform engineering, reproducibility, AI autonomy, deployment, estimator-hardening, and shadow-estimator work into a versioned release candidate intended for scientific replication and external software review.

This release is **not flight ready** and does not claim physical HIL, production-radio, tethered-flight, free-flight, or operational airworthiness validation.

## Major Highlights

### Estimator hardening

- fail-closed EKF validation and deterministic replay
- transactional estimator update behavior and covariance safety checks
- active/shadow estimator architecture with active authority preserved
- hardened shadow ESKF propagation and error-state handling
- stationary detection and automatic shadow-only ZUPT
- First-Estimate Jacobian support

### Visual/MSCKF shadow validation

- deterministic camera-state sliding window
- feature observation history and geometric initialization
- multi-view triangulation with baseline, depth, and reprojection gating
- shadow-only MSCKF feature residual/Jacobian construction
- FEJ-aware null-space projection
- deterministic stacked measurement formation
- configurable statistical/innovation gating
- transactional Joseph-form shadow correction

### Sustained readiness soak validation

The release candidate includes a fail-closed sustained readiness monitor on top of the existing point-in-time estimator-promotion readiness gate.

Promotion readiness must remain healthy for a configurable number of consecutive observations. Readiness progress resets when the underlying gate regresses, including conditions represented by the current readiness contract such as stale or dropped measurements, processing failures, estimator-health degradation, or divergence.

The monitor provides readiness evidence only. It does **not** promote an estimator, switch active authority, or activate flight behavior.

## Safety Boundary

v2.0.0 intentionally excludes automatic estimator authority promotion and rollback. Those mechanisms should be developed and validated in a separate post-v2.0.0 stage/branch so the release baseline remains reproducible and fail-closed.

Do not use simulation, playback, CI, or bench-only evidence as proof of real-world flight performance.

## Known Limitations

- physical HIL incomplete
- no production-radio qualification
- no validated multi-drone tethered/free-flight deployment
- no operational airworthiness certification
- no automatic estimator authority promotion
- no claim of peer-reviewed publication acceptance solely from this release

## Follow-On Direction

The recommended next estimator milestone after v2.0.0 is an explicitly controlled **Estimator Authority Promotion & Rollback** stage with operator-controlled promotion, health/divergence rollback, anti-flapping/hysteresis, deterministic replay evidence, telemetry, and fail-closed restart behavior.

Other roadmap items include physical HIL, multi-drone bench/tethered validation, production-radio testing, CBOR transport HIL, protobuf experiments, packet-signing/replay-nonce hardening, post-quantum authentication experiments, and formal safety-invariant verification.
